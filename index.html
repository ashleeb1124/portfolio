01Meet Ashlee

Deep enough to own the work. Senior enough to lead it.

I started as an HR Assistant on the floor of a Fortune 500 Coca-Cola bottling plant and worked my way up to running it. That shaped everything since: go to the floor, find the real constraint, fix the process, make the fix repeatable.

Since then I've built the infrastructure that took a solar construction company from 5 contracts to ~40 concurrent projects, opened 20 clinics, scaled a behavioral health organization from about $500K to $24M in annualized revenue across 10 facilities, and launched a geriatric medical practice from zero.

HR has been part of every role I've held. I've run HR as a department of one, and I've led HR teams of up to 14. Operations, healthcare or people, the discipline is the same: clear ownership, good data and systems that outlast the person who built them.
