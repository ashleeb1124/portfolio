HOW TO PUBLISH YOUR SITE ON NETLIFY

1. Unzip this file. You'll get a folder called "ashlee-bryant-site".
   Keep everything inside it together: index.html, ashlee-bryant.jpg,
   the 3 CV PDFs and the "work" folder.

2. Go to app.netlify.com and log in.

3. Click your site: ashleebryantbrand

4. Open the "Deploys" tab.

5. Drag the whole "ashlee-bryant-site" folder (the folder, not the zip)
   onto the box that says "Drag and drop your site output folder here".

6. Wait about 30 seconds, then visit https://ashleebryantbrand.netlify.app
   and refresh. Test the "Download my CV" menu and click a work sample.

TO SWAP OR ADD A WORK SAMPLE LATER
- Put the new image in the "work" folder.
- Send it to Claude and ask to add it to the gallery, or copy one of the
  <figure class="g"> blocks in index.html and change the file names and text.

TO UPDATE A CV
- Replace the PDF in the folder with the new one, using the exact same
  file name, then drag the folder into Netlify again.
