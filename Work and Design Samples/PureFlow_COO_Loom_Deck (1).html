07 · SEC Solar Case Study

Problem → Fix → Permanent Solution

What broke

Growth outpaced the operating process.

~5 Florida contracts became ~40 concurrent projects across the Southeast, and onboarding had become increasingly ad hoc.

Business impact

More friction. Less visibility.

Project launches were harder to coordinate, while leadership lacked consistent real-time visibility into capacity and performance.

What I did

I took ownership of the operating transition.

Standardized onboarding, coordinated cross-functional teams, and built repeatable workflows, reporting, and accountability.

Root cause

The model was still built for the old scale.

The business had expanded faster than the processes supporting it.

Permanent fix

Standardize → Systemize → Report → Replicate

Repeatable contract onboarding, reporting structures, real-time dashboards, and clear accountability measures.

Result

25% faster project timelines

40% lower payroll-related administrative costs.

The lesson
When growth creates complexity, adding more people is not always the answer. Sometimes the answer is building a better operating system.
