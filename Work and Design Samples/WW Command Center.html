Operating system

What I would instrument first

The dashboard is the output. The real work is defining ownership, clean data, thresholds, and actions behind each metric.

01

Studio Scorecards

Revenue, bookings, utilization, labor, inventory, rebook, reviews, payroll exceptions, and profit by location.

02

Call Center

Lead capture, speed to answer, booking conversion, follow-up completion, show rate, and lost-call recovery.

03

People + Payroll

Open roles, time to fill, 90-day retention, training completion, staffing gaps, payroll exceptions, and productivity.

04

Inventory

Par levels, days supply, physical-to-system variance, replenishment timing, shrinkage, and stock-out prevention.

05

Facilities + Vendors

Open tickets, aging, SLA performance, recurring repairs, location spend, preventive maintenance, and vendor scorecards.

06

Weekly Cadence

Exception-led operating review, explicit owners, deadlines, decisions, and closed-loop follow-up without founder dependence.

Not a website critique. These are the operational checkpoints I would connect to growth, local visibility, and booking conversion.

A

Location Page QA

B

Booking Journey

Measure clicks → bookings → arrivals → treatments by source, location, device, offer, and time of day.

C

Local Reputation

Monitor review velocity, rating, response time, recurring themes, and location-level recovery actions.
